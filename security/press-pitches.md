# Pitches that can produce Wikipedia sources

Wikipedia needs two or three articles **about** Rishithej, written by people
with no connection to him. These three emails are the shortest path to that.
**Rishi sends them himself**, from his own address: editors respond to the
person, not to someone writing on his behalf.

Rules for every pitch:
- Offer a story, never pay for one. A "sponsored", "brand spotlight" or
  "partner content" piece cannot be cited on Wikipedia and makes real coverage
  look suspect.
- Claim only what no company website contradicts. Titles at ML Dynamic and
  FirstSecure are unresolved (intake form section 05), so these pitches avoid
  them, and avoid DPIIT claims until each one is checked.
- Attach two or three photos and give a phone number. Answer within hours.

I am happy to do a short interview, write a few paragraphs, or send photographs from graduation and from my work.

With thanks,
Bollam Rishithej
[phone]
https://bollamrishithej.com

Why first: a page on le.ac.uk is independent, verifiable and confirms the
degree, which also corrects the AI answers still repeating the wrong one.

**To:** editor@telanganatoday.com (better still, find the reporter whose byline
appears on recent startup and business stories and write to them by name)

```text
Dear Editor,

I am a Hyderabad entrepreneur attending Phase 1 of the 140th Canton Fair in Guangzhou (15 to 19 October), travelling with a group of businesspeople from the Telugu states.

The story I would like to offer is not about buying from China. I co-founded VidyuthLabs, a Hyderabad company building portable electrochemical sensing instruments, and making hardware here is the problem I am trying to solve. I can share photographs from the fair floor and am available for an interview when I return to Hyderabad on [date].

My notes from the first day are here: https://bollamrishithej.com/blog/canton-fair-day-one

Regards,
Bollam Rishithej
[phone]
```

Send while the fair is running (until 19 October); the news value fades after.

## After a piece is published

1. Save the URL and the date; take a PDF copy in case the page moves.
2. Add it to the intake form (section 01), or tell us directly.
3. `node intake/readiness.mjs` turns it into the citation for the draft.
4. Two or three such pieces, and the draft can be submitted.
