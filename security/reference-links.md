## 6. Contradictions these pages expose

1. **ML Dynamic Solutions title.** The company's own site lists him as **CEO**,
   names someone else as Founder, and someone else as Managing Director.
   His site, the og:image card, the Wikipedia draft and CLAUDE.md all say
   **Founder & Managing Director**. Emergera's page says Managing Director.
   CLAUDE.md's own rule is that the company's own listing wins.
   CEO is also a Section 203 whole-time KMP office, so "one KMP office"
   still holds either way; what changes is the title and the Founder claim.
2. **FirstSecure.ai title.** The company lists him as Co-Founder & Chief
   Marketing Officer. His site says Co-Founder & Director.
3. **What ML Dynamic does.** Its site says AI healthcare revenue-cycle
   management; Emergera's page and his site say enterprise AI.
4. **Search engines still show the old description.** The search result for
   bollamrishithej.com still reads "CEO of the ML Group of Companies" and a
   master's "from London" — text the site no longer contains. Only a Search
   Console re-index request fixes that.
