'''Rishithej Bollam''' (born 22 September 1999), also written '''Bollam Rishithej''', is an Indian company director and technology entrepreneur based in [[Hyderabad]], [[Telangana]].<ref name="SOURCE-1" /> He is the founder and managing director of ML Dynamic Solutions, and holds directorships across companies working in artificial intelligence, computer vision, electrochemical sensing and industrial operations.<ref name="SOURCE-1" />

== Education ==

Bollam holds a Master of Science in Entrepreneurship and Innovation Management from the [[University of Leicester]] in the United Kingdom. His postgraduate work addressed a business model for converting plastic waste into fuel.<ref name="SOURCE-2" />

== Career ==

Bollam is the founder and managing director of ML Dynamic Solutions, a company working on artificial intelligence applied to healthcare revenue cycle management.<ref name="SOURCE-1" />

He is a co-founder and director of VidyuthLabs, which develops portable electrochemical sensing instruments, and of Predictera, a computer vision company. Several of the companies are recognised as startups under the Indian government's Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade framework.<ref name="SOURCE-3" />

He is also a co-founder and chief technology officer of Emergera.ai, a company registered in the United States.<ref name="SOURCE-4" />
